How to play / 玩法说明

Click or tap to reveal a square. A number counts the mines in its eight neighbouring squares. Right-click to place or remove a flag. On a phone, turn on Flag mode to flag squares, then turn it off to reveal them.

点击翻开方格，数字表示周围八格中的雷数。右键插旗或取消；手机上可先开启“插旗”模式，标记后再关闭。首次翻开的方格及其周围一圈不会有雷。翻开所有无雷方格即获胜，无需标出全部地雷。

Click a revealed number after placing that many neighbouring flags to open its remaining neighbours. Incorrect flags can still lead to a mine!

Keyboard: Tab into the board, use arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to use the selected mode, and F to toggle a flag. Best times are saved in this browser when storage is available.
